The keeper stays home

Wills never leaves his box in any of the eighteen. He shades toward the ball side to cover the near post, and he is the spare man behind the low block — but the empty net isn't a risk worth taking at this age.

02

No ball: slide, keep the width

Wyatt presses the ball on his own. Behind him the eight shuffle a few metres toward it but hold their spacing rather than collapsing into a knot — at 9v9 a squeezed block just gets played around the outside of.

03

Our ball: the far winger holds

When we attack down one side, Nathaniel or Myland stays glued to the opposite touchline doing nothing. That is the job. He is what makes the switch a goal instead of a pass.
