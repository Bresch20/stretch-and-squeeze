Build — centre

Shaded green is the box the eight outfielders occupy — Wills sits outside it because he never leaves his area. Grey marks the square the ball is in. The out-of-possession shapes come from your own sketch: a back three, Coen screening in front, a line of three, and Wyatt pressing alone.

01

The keeper stays home

Wills never leaves his box in any of the eighteen. He shades toward the ball side to cover the near post, and he is the spare man behind the low block — but the empty net isn't a risk worth taking at this age.

02

No ball: slide, keep the width

Wyatt presses the ball on his own. Behind him the eight shuffle a few metres toward it but hold their spacing rather than collapsing into a knot — at 9v9 a squeezed block just gets played around the outside of.

03

Our ball: the far winger holds

When we attack down one side, Nathaniel or Myland stays glued to the opposite touchline doing nothing. That is the job. He is what makes the switch a goal instead of a pass.

Editing: all eighteen zones are already filled in and stored separately — nine with the ball, nine without — so there is a real board waiting behind every square of the grid. The ball is draggable too — it picks up a dashed ring in edit mode, and stays inside its own square, so you can put it on the touchline or at a player's feet rather than dead centre. Hit Edit shapes, then walk the grid with Next and Back — they step one square at a time, all eighteen in order, so you can fix each one as you reach it. Play tour does the same walk on a timer, and any drag pauses it. Moving one zone never disturbs another, and a square turns green once you have changed it. Mirror to other side copies the zone you are on to the opposite lane, flipped, so you only ever have to draw one side.
